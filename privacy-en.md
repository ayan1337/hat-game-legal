# Privacy Policy

_Updated: November 19, 2026_

## In short

The Hat is a free party game. We don't collect your data: the app has no accounts, ads, analytics or servers of its own. Everything you type stays on your phone.

## What stays on your phone

To make the game work, the app keeps on your phone: player names and cards of an unfinished game (erased when the game ends); settings - language, theme, volume and chosen categories; the random cards of the last two games, so the 🎲 doesn't repeat itself; your own categories with their words - until you delete them. None of this is ever sent anywhere. The app also remembers that it has already asked “First time playing?”.

## Permissions

The app uses the phone's motion sensor (gyroscope) only to hide the word if the phone is turned sharply. Sensor readings are not stored or sent. The app also uses sound and vibration. It does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

## Sharing

We don't share or sell any data - we simply don't have it. The app works offline. If purchases ever appear, they are handled by the App Store and Google Play: we never see your card details. The Share button on the results screen makes a picture of the game results right on your phone and opens the phone’s share menu. The picture goes only where you send it, and only after you tap - we never receive it. If you save it to Photos, your phone does that itself.

## Security

The app's data is protected the same way as everything else on your phone: by your passcode, Face ID or fingerprint.

## Your rights

We don't keep any data about you, so there is nothing to request or delete from us. You delete what the app stores yourself: your own categories right in the app, everything else by deleting the app.

## Children

The game is made for a general audience, not specifically for children. Kids are welcome to play together with adults. The app collects no data about anyone, children included. Parents should know that players write the words on the cards themselves.

## Ads

There are no ads in the app right now. If ads appear, we will update this policy first: we'll name the ad networks and the data they receive (for example, the device's advertising ID). On iPhone the app will then ask for permission to track - you can say no and still play.

## Changes

If anything related to data is added to the app, we will update this policy and the date at the top in advance.
